# Magic Cursor for Windows 11

**Created by Yathin Pedamallu**


**Watch what it does:** [Magic Cursor video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YV_6v9oF0uc)

Circle anything on your Windows 11 screen and ask Gemini about it, in any app, without opening a browser.

**Download:** [MagicCursor.exe](https://github.com/praveenp-works/magic_cursor/releases/latest/download/MagicCursor.exe)

**Latest version:** v77 · Updated October 27, 2026

## What it does

- **Select anything** – drag a box around any part of your screen (a chart, an error, a photo, a page) and ask about it.
- **Gemini answers** – detailed answers with follow-up questions in the same chat.
- **Make images** – ask it to create or edit a picture, then save or copy it.
- **Talk to it** – voice typing with the mic button, and answers read out loud if you want.
- **Saved chats** – reopen your recent chats.
- **Light and dark theme.**

## Install (one time, about 1 minute)

1. **Download [MagicCursor.exe](https://github.com/praveenp-works/magic_cursor/releases/latest/download/MagicCursor.exe)** and double-click it.
   No Python or other setup needed.
   If Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
2. The first time, it asks for your **Gemini API key**. Get one free at https://aistudio.google.com/apikey and paste it in.

## How to open it

- Click the small round Gemini button at the top right of your screen.
- Press **Ctrl + Shift + Space**.
- Shake your mouse back and forth **6 times quickly**.
- Say **"Hey Cursor"** or **"Magic Cursor"**.

Then drag a box around anything and type your question.

## Good to know

- **Starts with Windows.** Turn this off in the gear menu, or in Windows **Settings > Apps > Startup**.
- **Updates itself.** It checks for a new version every 6 hours and installs it when the chat is closed. You can also tap the gear and choose **Check for updates**.
- **Your key stays on your PC.** Screenshots you select are sent to Google Gemini only to answer your question.
- **To quit:** open it, tap the gear, then tap **Quit**.
- Making images may need billing turned on for your Gemini key.
